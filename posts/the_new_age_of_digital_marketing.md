## Digital Marketing Fundamentals

When it comes to attracting leads, engaging customers, and gaining that competitive advantage, digital marketing is vital for your business and brand awareness. While most brands have a website, those that don't typically maintain a social media presence or implement a digital ad strategy. 

> Consumers now expect and rely on digital content and marketing as a way to learn about brands.

To be competitive as a business, you'll need to embrace digital marketing. With so many options and strategies available, digital marketing allows you to get creative and experiment with a variety of marketing tactics — no matter your budget. In this lesson, learn how to identify the digital marketing strategies and tactics that work best for your business and your customers.

## Building Ethical Digital Marketing Strategies

Ethics form the foundation of successful digital marketing, helping brands build trust and lasting relationships with their audience. Consumers expect more than just great products, they expect brands to respect their privacy, communicate honestly, and honor inclusivity. In this lesson, you’ll explore in detail the critical components of ethical digital marketing from privacy and data protection to honest communication and fair competition. 

> You’ll learn how to safely collect, store, and use customer data through transparent consent practices and secure data handling. 

You’ll explore why truthful marketing is crucial for brand credibility, examine the impact of misleading messages, and distinguish between ethical and unethical advertising practices. By the end of this lesson, you’ll have a clear understanding of the values and daily actions that set your brand apart. You’ll be ready to apply ethical standards in your marketing campaigns, helping you to grow trustworthy relationships with your audience.
