1. Project Overview & Objective

The goal of this project was to transform raw transactional sales data into actionable business insights. I built a comprehensive E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard in Tableau to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, geographic trends, product performance, seasonality, and shipping operations.

Skills Demonstrated: Data Cleaning, Data Validation, KPI Development, Tableau Dashboard Design, Business Intelligence, Customer & Geographic Analytics, Time-Series Analysis, and Data Storytelling.

2. Data Collection & Custom KPIs

The dataset utilized consisted of granular transactional data, including Order Dates, Ship Modes, Customer Segments, Regions, and Product Categories. To drive the dashboards, I developed several calculated fields in Tableau:

KPI Metric Tableau Calculation
Total Sales SUM([Sales])
Total Orders COUNTD([Order ID])
Total Customers COUNTD([Customer ID])
Average Order Value (AOV) SUM([Sales]) / COUNTD([Order ID])
Average Shipping Time AVG(DATEDIFF('day',[Order Date],[Ship Date]))

3. Dashboard Architecture & Storytelling

To avoid clutter and ensure a clear business narrative, the project was strategically organized into six focused dashboard modules.

Executive Summary & Product Performance

High-level KPIs outlining overall sales trends, revenue generation by category, and top/bottom performing products.

  • Executive View: Answers macro-level questions regarding overall business health, category generation, and regional contributions. Features core KPIs (Total Sales, Orders, AOV).
  • Product Analysis: Drills down into Categories and Sub-Categories. Identifies the Top 10 revenue-driving products and the Bottom 10 underperforming products to help optimize inventory.

Geographic Analysis & Customer Insights

Map-based visualizations and segmentation charts highlighting market strength and high-value customers.

  • Geographic Mapping: Highlights the strongest markets by breaking down sales by Region, State, and City.
  • Customer Insights: Analyzes which customer segments contribute the most revenue, identifies the highest-value VIP customers, and tracks the ratio of new versus repeat customers.

Time Trends, Seasonality & Shipping Operations

Analysis of recurring demand patterns and logistical efficiency across shipping modes.

  • Time Trends: Utilizes Year-over-Year growth analysis and monthly trends to identify recurring periods of high seasonal demand.
  • Operations & Logistics: Evaluates average shipping times by Region and Ship Mode, uncovering which methods are fastest and where supply chain bottlenecks occur.

4. Technical Challenges & Solutions

  • Challenge 1: Inflated Order Counts. Initially, total orders were calculated using COUNT(Order ID), which caused inflated numbers because a single order could contain multiple products across several rows.
    Solution: Transitioned to using COUNTD(Order ID) to accurately count unique orders.
  • Challenge 2: Negative Shipping Time Values. Shipping dashboards displayed negative values (some up to -700 days). Investigation revealed that some Order and Ship dates were accidentally modified during raw data prep (e.g., Order Date = 2025, Ship Date = 2023).
    Solution: Validated the date fields, cleaned invalid date entries, and recalculated the DATEDIFF metrics to restore accuracy.
  • Challenge 3: Lack of Dashboard Storytelling. The initial iteration featured too many charts on a single page, diluting the business narrative.
    Solution: Reorganized the project into six distinct, logical modules (Executive, Product, Geo, Customer, Time, Operations), massively improving usability and analytical flow.

5. Key Business Insights

  • Revenue Concentration: Sales are heavily concentrated within a few specific product categories, indicating areas where marketing budget should be prioritized.
  • The Pareto Principle in Action: A relatively small group of high-value, repeat customers contributes a disproportionately large share of total sales.
  • Logistics: While "Standard Class" is the most commonly used shipping method, shipping efficiency varies noticeably across different regions, highlighting opportunities for localized supply chain optimizations.
  • Seasonality: Clear, recurring seasonal trends dictate demand spikes, providing a blueprint for when to ramp up inventory and promotional efforts.
